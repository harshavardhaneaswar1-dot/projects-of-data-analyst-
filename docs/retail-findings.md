# Project 1 — Retail (Superstore) Findings

Dataset: `data/samplesuperstore.csv` — 10,194 order lines (US Superstore demo). Report: `reports/project1.pbix`.

## Headlines

- Total Sales **≈ $2.33M**; Total Profit **≈ $292K**.
- **Technology** is the top category for both sales and profit.
- **West ≈ 31.8% of sales**, the leading region.
- **California and New York** are the top states by sales; **Texas records a net loss** despite high volume.
- Heavy discounts (>~20%) cluster with negative-profit orders.

## Takeaways

1. Protect Technology margins; review Furniture pricing/discounts.
2. Fix Texas: cut deep discounts on loss-making sub-categories.
3. Push West-coast winners into Central/South assortments.
